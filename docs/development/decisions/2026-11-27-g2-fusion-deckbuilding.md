# G2 two-sided Fusion and dual-faction construction

Toy Archive release 2026-11-27-pilot.18 (7492525) uses the previously captured Fusion physical tables and pinned Card Archive to review 54 two-sided pairings (44 Armored Alliance, 10 Legends, 108 card records). Exact listed face filenames, both face stats, physical faction identity and two BakuCore indicators agree. Twelve front-facing factions differ from the first physical label faction. Three reviewed pairs have only one distinct fused faction. Fifteen physical Fusion variants remain unmatched because of incomplete or conflicting evidence. The 1,620 physical variants, 747 products, 626 ordinary linked variants and all IDs are unchanged. There are now 680 exact Character-linked physical variants including Fusion. The audit counts both exact faces as referenced, and the Toy Archive displays both linked faces independently.

Deckbuilder release 2026-11-27-deck.7 (a636847) offers a sixth opt-in rule profile, g2-all-seasons-fusion-v1. The original five format IDs/scopes remain unchanged. The [official January 2020 Glossary](https://intl2.bakugan.com/sites/bakugan/files/2020-01/Glossary.pdf) supplies the 3-team, 6-core and 40-main-deck baseline; a [firsthand report of the 2019 Spin Master Invitational preview](https://thegeekiary.com/bakugan-invitational-tournament-2019/74080) documents the later dual-faction requirement. The [Spin Master 2020 fact sheet](https://assets.ctfassets.net/r3qu44etwf9a/3XNzJ1bw124vHPfTmKV2rK/c774c86b453f75a5548007135ca789fd/Bakugan_2020_Fact_Sheets.pdf) establishes the physical Fusion product. Complete later official rules were not located, so the new format is a limited, conservative pilot. Both distinct factions of a dual-faction deck card need confirmed representation in the starting team. A Fusion contributes only the printed unfused faction and starting core indicators.

The new profile allows Flip Heroes and dual-faction Action, Hero, Flip, Evo and Gear records. Both Fusion sides are linked and displayed; only the front ID represents the Character requirement, with the maximum of the two recorded face quantities used for conservative ownership comparison, never their sum. No new storage schema, collection/catalog card IDs, account services, Cloudflare costs or shared stylesheet changes. Eleven exact revision-bound printings with effects referring to your own team's Fusion members warn if all three selected confirmed slots lack Fusion; warnings are not legality errors. Activation conditions and in-game state are not simulated. Remaining special treatments, Epic pairs and dedicated Geogan/Nanogan cards require further review.

Verification: all 141 aggregated automated tests, repository invariant checks and 63 local browser smoke checks passed, including full checked construction with real pinned Fusion/dual-faction fixtures, front and back links, exact starting cores, 320px layout, persistence, and unchanged guest inventory.
