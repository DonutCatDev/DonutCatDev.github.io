# G2 Geogan and Nanogan support construction

Toy Archive release 2026-11-27-pilot.19 (4761d7c) adds 149 exact package-listed support associations, each binding the specific physical variant, package proof, source revision/hash and pinned card page/printing. This covers 97 of the 98 Geogan cards and 52 of the 58 Nanogan cards in the Card Archive, corresponding to 97 of 104 physical Geogan and 52 of 63 physical Nanogan. Existing ordinary Character links, Fusion pairs, products, and physical/collection IDs do not change. Six Nanogan cards without an exact package link have name/faction-matching physical candidates for manual review. Aquos Manticore has a card but no reviewed corresponding physical Geogan identity; it remains unknown rather than fabricating a toy. Physical Gear is not associated with random included Gear cards.

Deckbuilder release 2026-11-27-deck.9 (eaff2c8) adds a seventh opt-in format, g2-all-seasons-support-v1. The official January 2020 Glossary establishes the ordinary three-team/six-core/forty-card baseline. The [official Evolutions tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMxcrkp_YA8) and [official Geogan product description](https://intl2.bakugan.com/en/toys/778988395455/bakugan-auratoa-2-inch-tall-geogan-rising-collectible-action-figure-and-trading-card) provide support-card context. The stored community Geogan (851697) and Nanogan (866058) articles supply the later accessory and separate card-zone construction details; a complete later official written rulebook was not located. The result is a construction pilot, not tournament certification.

Geogan belong in the main 40, with the existing copy and faction checks and energy analytics; one package-linked physical option auto-selects when unique. Manual alternatives remain uncertain, and a missing/invalid selection receives an explicit check. The missing collection list includes a minimum one physical Geogan per selected exact identity, not one per card copy. Nanogan cards occupy an optional 0-3-card zone outside the 40 and retain starting-team faction checks. A physical Nanogan selection is optional and does not become a missing required item. No additional Nanogan duplication restriction is asserted on incomplete sources. Fifteen revision-bound core recipes show an advisory when no recipe is covered by selected starting core symbols; actual in-game activation is not simulated. Later core gains can satisfy recipes.

The deck draft schema adds an optional supportToyId field and the nanogan zone while preserving old entries and format IDs. Backups, revision-locked saves, and format-switch retention remain intact. No Collection Manager, Card Archive, account schema, backend, or visual theme change is required.

Verification: 144 automated tests, repository lint, and 66 browser smoke checks passed, including 40-card and optional-zone validation, exact/manual support selection, core advisory, persistence at 320px, package-linked archive detail and unchanged guest inventory.
